# Itervox Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

This agreement is for companies and other organizations (a "Corporation")
whose employees or contractors contribute to Itervox (the "Project") as part
of their work. It lets the Corporation authorize those people to contribute,
and gives the Project Owner the same license the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md) gives. **The Corporation keeps the copyright in
everything its people contribute.** What it gives us is a broad license to
use it.

Each individual who contributes should still sign off every commit under the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](DCO).

## How to sign

1. Fill in the signature block and Schedule A at the end of this document.
   The person signing must be authorized to bind the Corporation.
2. Sign it (a handwritten signature on a scanned copy, or an
   electronic signature, are both fine) and save it as a PDF.
3. Email the PDF to **vlad@novicklabs.com** with the subject
   "Itervox Corporate CLA — [Corporation name]".
4. We will acknowledge receipt and record the GitHub accounts listed in
   Schedule A so the CLA check on their pull requests passes. Until that is
   done, a listed employee can also sign the [Individual CLA](CLA.md) by
   commenting on their pull request.

To add or remove people later, the Point of Contact sends an updated
Schedule A to the same address. You do not need to sign the whole agreement
again.

- **"Corporation"** or **"You"** means the legal entity named in the signature
  block, together with every entity that controls it, is controlled by it, or
  is under common control with it. "Control" means owning 50% or more of the
  outstanding shares or voting securities, or having the power to direct the
  entity's management, by contract or otherwise.
- **"Designated Employees"** means the employees, contractors and other people
  listed in Schedule A, as updated by the Point of Contact from time to time.
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any
  change or addition to an existing work, that a Designated Employee
  intentionally Submits to Us for inclusion in the Project or in any other

- **"Submit"** means any form of electronic, verbal or written communication

  comments and messages on the Project's repositories, issue trackers, mailing
  lists and chat channels, made for the purpose of discussing or improving the
  Project. It does not include communication conspicuously marked, in writing,

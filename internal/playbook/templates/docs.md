This issue changes documentation.

1. **Know the reader.** Say who reads the page and what they need to do after reading it.
2. **Check every claim against the code.** Run each command and example you document and paste real output.
3. **Keep it short:** plain words, short sentences, one idea per paragraph.
4. **Link** to the related pages instead of repeating them.
5. In your handoff, list the pages you changed and the commands you ran to check them.
